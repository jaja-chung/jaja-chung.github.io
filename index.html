從實驗室現場出發，用自動化工具改善重複作業

Starting from the lab floor, improving repetitive work with tools that automate it

我在醫學中心的分子病理實驗室工作，執行 NGS、FISH 等臨床分子檢驗，也負責試劑庫存管理、每月檢驗量與結果統計，以及各項行政作業。在日常工作中，我發現重複的資料整理、人工核對與文書處理需要投入不少時間，也容易發生疏漏。

I work in the molecular pathology laboratory of a medical center, running NGS, FISH and other clinical molecular assays, and I also look after reagent inventory, the monthly assay-volume and results statistics, and a range of administrative work. In the course of that work I found that the repetitive data handling, manual cross-checking and paperwork take up a considerable amount of time, and are where mistakes most easily slip through.

因此，我開始整理作業流程與需求，運用 AI 輔助開發工具，將重複步驟自動化，並加入資料檢查與異常提醒。目前已從零建立三套系統，並接手一套現行工具的功能擴充與維護，皆已應用於實際工作。

So I began setting out the processes and the requirements, then built tools with AI assistance that automate the repetitive steps and add data checks and alerts. Three systems have been built from scratch, and I extend and maintain a fourth that already existed; all four are in real use.

在實驗室工作與工具開發的過程中，我發現自己特別投入於釐清問題、分析資料，以及將改善方式落實到日常作業。我希望將這些經驗延伸至半導體製造領域，投入製程監控、異常分析與持續改善相關工作。

Through my laboratory work and tool development, I have found that I am particularly drawn to investigating problems, analyzing data and putting improvements into everyday practice. I hope to bring this experience to semiconductor manufacturing, contributing to process monitoring, problem analysis and continuous improvement.

PROJECT 02 資料整合｜報表自動化Data integration · reporting

分子檢驗月統計自動化Monthly Assay Statistics, Automated

每月需彙整各檢驗項目的檢體量、突變率與報告時效，原本仰賴人工整理、計算與逐項核對。我將資料整理、檢查與簡報製作串接為自動化流程，遇到必要資料缺漏時，暫停產出並提示補正。Every month the specimen volume, mutation rate and turnaround time for each assay have to be brought together — collated, calculated and cross-checked by hand. I joined the collation, the checks and the deck into one automated flow that pauses and asks for a fix when required data is missing.

代表成果Outcome 約 2 分鐘自動完成資料彙整與統計簡報產出。Data collation and the statistics deck are produced automatically in about 2 minutes.

PROJECT 03 文件產生｜行政流程自動化Documents · admin automation

報帳文件自動化Reimbursement Documents, Automated

每月報帳原本需依試算表逐案分組、計算金額，再填入三份固定格式的文件。我將案件整理、金額計算與文件製作整合為自動化流程，讓使用者先查看案件與金額，確認後再產出文件。Monthly billing used to mean grouping cases from a spreadsheet one by one, working out the amounts and filling in three fixed-format documents. I brought the grouping, the arithmetic and the document work into one automated flow, where the cases and the amounts are reviewed first and the documents produced once they are confirmed.

代表成果Outcome 一次整理請款資料，自動套用至多份文件並更新請款明細，降低重複計算與轉填的出錯風險。One pass over the billing data is applied to every document and written to the ledger, cutting the repeated arithmetic and re-typing where mistakes crept in.

PROJECT 04 功能擴充｜持續維護Feature extension · Ongoing maintenance

NGS 報告轉換工具NGS Report Conversion Tool

現行工具可讀取檢測廠商提供的報告，將內容自動帶入院內制式報告模板。原本需逐份處理報告，並將資料另行填入檢測統計表；我接手後新增批次處理與統計表匯出功能，並修正部分資料讀取與文字格式問題。The tool reads the reports supplied by the testing vendor and places their content into the hospital’s standard report template; before I extended it, each report had to be handled on its own and the data retyped into the test-statistics workbook. Since taking it over I have added batch processing and the workbook export, and fixed some of the data reading and text formatting.

代表成果Outcome 多份報告可一次轉換，解析結果也可加入既有檢測統計表，減少逐份操作與重複轉填。Several reports are converted in one pass and the parsed results added to an existing test-statistics workbook, cutting the case-by-case handling and the retyping.

我如何設計與改善自動化工具How I design and improve these tools

上述專案處理不同的作業需求。從資料檢查、人工確認到日常維護，以下是我在建置與改善工具時採用的做法。

The projects above answer different needs. From checking the data, through the judgements left to a person, to keeping things running — these are the practices I build and improve by.

01

把資料檢核放進流程Data checks built into the flow

在統計與文件產出前檢查必要資料，指出缺漏的紀錄與欄位，並依問題影響決定暫停產出、排除個案或提示確認，方便使用者回查與補正。Before the statistics or documents are produced, the required data is checked. Missing records and fields are named, and depending on what the problem affects, output is held, the case is left out of this run, or the user is asked to confirm — so it can be traced back and filled in.

02

保留需要人工判斷的環節Keep the steps that need a person

將規則明確的計算與整理交給程式；遇到資料不一致或超出既定規則的情況，則呈現相關資訊與提醒，讓使用者確認後再繼續。Calculations and collation that follow clear rules go to the program. Where the data disagrees with itself, or falls outside the rules, the relevant information and a warning are shown, and the user confirms before anything goes further.

03

將常用參數集中管理Keep the common settings in one place

將單價、契約項次與代碼對應等可設定內容集中管理，讓日常調整可透過設定表完成，減少直接修改程式的需求。The settings that get adjusted — unit prices, contract line items, code mappings — are kept together, so routine changes can be made in a settings sheet and fewer of them have to be made in the code.

04

從使用回饋追查與改善Trace it back from how it is used

從日常操作與使用者回饋發現問題，回查資料、作業規則與操作情境，調整後再核對結果，並更新相關說明與修改紀錄。Problems surface in daily use and in what users report. I trace them back through the data, the rules the work runs by and the situation they came up in, then check the result after the change and bring the notes and the record of what changed up to date.
